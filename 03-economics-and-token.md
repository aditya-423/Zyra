# 03. Economics and Token

## Protocol Economics
Zyra operates as an AI-driven trading assistant that translates natural language into on-chain strategies. The economic activity flows through two surfaces:

- **Retail Users** pay execution fees for trades (market, limit, DCA, multi-step strategies).  
- **B2B Partners** (DeFi protocols, dApps, and institutions) integrate Zyra via SDK/API and pay subscription or usage-based fees.  

All fees collected flow back into the Zyra token economy, where they are used to reward stakers, fund governance, and fuel deflationary mechanisms.

## Token Role
Zyra introduces a native utility and governance token (“Zyra token”), designed to align users, developers, and institutional partners:

- **Transaction Fuel**: Required for executing advanced strategies or API-based institutional flows.  
- **Fee Reductions**: Users who stake tokens benefit from reduced execution costs.  
- **Feature Unlocks**: Holding tokens unlocks premium features such as complex multi-step strategies, analytics, and early access to new modules.  
- **Governance**: Token holders propose and vote on new strategies, fee models, and treasury use.  
- **Deflationary Mechanism**: A percentage of all fees collected in Zyra tokens are burned, permanently reducing supply.  

This model ensures persistent demand for the token as Zyra adoption grows, while gradually reducing circulating supply.

**Economic Incentive**:  
As adoption grows, users pay Zyra token for advanced strategies and integrations. Staking incentivizes holding, while the burn mechanism ensures that higher protocol usage translates directly into value accrual for long-term token holders.
